The 50 answer calls are distinct recorded performances. Some share a
syllable type. The stored WAV files retain their original pitch and speed.
File processing consists of cropping, peak normalization, 2 ms edge fades
and padding answer clips to 115 ms. At playback, QuizChirp applies a random
gain of -3 to +2 dB and a pitch/speed shift of up to +/-0.8 semitones,
with a peak headroom limit. Each answer repeats the same processed note
with fixed pauses. These playback changes do not modify the source WAVs.
tools/sound_clips.csv lists the source file, annotated syllable, original
32 kHz sample interval (end exclusive) and the RMS level difference
between the syllable and the adjacent 6 ms on either side, in decibels.
Pass all four archives to tools/build_sounds.py to reproduce the WAVs.
